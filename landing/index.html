Humanity has been writing itself notes for three thousand years.

Proverbs are how communities remember what works: when to trust, when to wait, when many hands help and when too many cooks spoil it. The Wisdom Extractor is an open research instrument that maps this knowledge across cultures — and everyone can help.

What your judgments do

Each "same idea / different idea" verdict trains and audits the alignment of proverbs across languages. Judgments are aggregated with a reliability model, feed the clustering, and are published (anonymously) as open scientific data.

Open by design

Code, corpus, and annotations are public: the software and data carry citable DOIs, and every number in the research is reproducible by one script.
