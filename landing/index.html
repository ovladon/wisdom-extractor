An open research project · Constanța, Romania

Humanity has been writing itself notes for three thousand years.

Proverbs are how communities remember what works: whom to trust, when to wait, when many hands help — and when too many cooks spoil it. We gathered tens of thousands of them, from a Scottish collection printed in 1663 to sayings still traded today, and mapped where the same wisdom appears across languages, continents, and centuries.

🌍 Explore the living map 🧭 Play — judge a pair of proverbs

The map: pick any motif and watch its trail across cultures — drag the time slider to replay its documented record through the centuries. The game: thirty seconds on your phone, and the map literally redraws because of you.

"Where there is smoke, there is fire."

Found in 17 cultures, from Spain to Vietnam, across at least five language families.

Why your thirty seconds matter

Machines can gather proverbs, but only people can judge whether two sayings from different worlds carry the same idea. Every judgment you make is weighed, cross-checked against other players, and folded into the map — and published (anonymously) as open scientific data that researchers anywhere can build on.

1 · You judge

Two sayings, side by side, in plain English. Same rule? Same advice? Just the same theme? You decide.

2 · The map learns

Judgments steer how proverbs cluster across cultures — and audit the algorithms that do it.

3 · Science keeps it

Everything becomes citable open data with a DOI. Your nickname stays on the leaderboard; the data stays anonymous.

Built in the open

📖 Peer-reviewed method

The extraction and comparison method behind the map was presented at the ConsILR 2025 conference on linguistic resources.

🗃️ Citable open data

The corpus and all anonymized judgments are archived with a DOI on Zenodo, free for any researcher to use.

⚙️ Public code

Every version of the software is on GitHub and archived with a software DOI — anyone can rebuild this map from scratch.
